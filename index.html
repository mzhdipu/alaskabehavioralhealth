Who we are

Integrated behavioral healthcare for every stage of life

As one of Alaska’s largest community behavioral health centers, we bring therapy, psychiatry and primary care together so every client gets coordinated, whole-person care. Our clinicians are trained in trauma-informed, evidence-based practices, and our doors are open to everyone.

  • Therapy, psychiatry and primary care under one roof
  • Trauma-informed, evidence-based treatment for all ages
  • Clinics in Anchorage, Wasilla and Fairbanks, plus telehealth statewide
  • No waitlist — first appointments usually within two weeks

Getting started

Your first step is simple

Becoming a client takes just a few minutes. Our team will guide you through every step.

1. Call or walk in

Call 907-563-1000 and choose your region, or visit any clinic Monday through Friday, 8am–5pm.

2. Complete your paperwork

Submit new client forms securely online, or arrive 30 minutes early to complete them at your first visit.

3. Meet your care team

There is no waitlist. First appointments are usually scheduled within two weeks, often sooner.

Paying for Services

For service members, veterans & families

The Steven A. Cohen Military Family Clinic

Confidential, evidence-based care for post-9/11 veterans, active-duty service members, National Guard and Reserves, and their families — regardless of discharge status, role in uniform, combat history or ability to pay.

  • Therapy for PTSD, depression, anxiety, sleep and transition challenges
  • Couples, child and family therapy
  • Case management and local resource connection
  • Secure telehealth anywhere in Alaska
