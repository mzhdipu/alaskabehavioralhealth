About Alaska Behavioral Health

Wellness for everyone, for more than 50 years

We are a community behavioral health center dedicated to ending the stigma around mental health and helping Alaskans of every age live well.

Who we are

Caring for Alaskans with a full range of mental health needs

Alaska Behavioral Health serves children and adults with a wide range of mental health needs, including children with severe emotional disturbance and adults with serious mental illness, with or without co-occurring substance use.

Our clinicians, medical providers, case managers and peer support specialists work side by side so each client receives coordinated, whole-person care. With clinics in Anchorage, Wasilla and Fairbanks and telehealth across the state, we make high-quality care easier to reach for families in every corner of Alaska.

Our values

The principles that guide our care

Six values shape how we show up for our clients, their families and our communities every day.

Community

We are part of the neighborhoods we serve and invest in stronger, healthier Alaska communities.

Access

Walk-ins are welcome, there is no waitlist, and no one is turned away because of inability to pay.

Relationships

Healing happens through trusting relationships with clients, families, partners and each other.

Integrity

We act with honesty, protect client privacy and hold ourselves to the highest professional standards.

Needs

Care is shaped around each person's unique needs, culture, strengths and goals.

Growth

We support recovery and growth for our clients, and ongoing learning for our staff.

Recognition

Trusted, certified and accredited

Certified Community Behavioral Health Clinic

As a CCBHC — one of only two in Alaska as of July 2022 — we offer comprehensive, coordinated care that meets rigorous federal standards.

National Child Traumatic Stress Network Partner

Our Alaska Child Trauma Center joined the NCTSN in 2005 as Alaska's first network site, advancing trauma care for children statewide.

Accredited by the Council on Accreditation

Independent accreditation confirms our commitment to quality, safety and best practices in every program.

Need help right now?

Call or text 988 any time, day or night. Text HOME to 741-741 to reach a crisis counselor. For a medical or mental health emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room.
